Questions

Asked and answered.

The app

What is IMAT6?

IMAT6 is an IMAT preparation app for self-study. It turns the IMAT syllabus into a path of lessons, practice questions, trap questions and timed mock tests, and runs on iPad, iPhone and Mac.

Who wrote the theory?

Mufaddal, a cancer biologist and medical-entrance tutor of seven years, wrote the Biology theory himself from his own notes, built around the Pearson Biology textbook and A-level resources.

What does the Biology question bank include?

Biology is complete: 7 units, 53 levels, 728 practice questions with an explanation for every option, 714 IMAT trap questions, and a 23-question Biology mock in 39 minutes.

What about Chemistry, Physics, Maths and Logic?

Chemistry lessons are in place across its six units, with practice questions on the way. Physics, Maths & Logic, and Reading & Comprehension come next, each with its own timed mock.

What is an IMAT trap?

A question written around a mistake the IMAT tends to test, such as assuming two isomers behave identically. Each trap explains why every option is right or wrong.

Studying & devices

How does negative marking work?

The IMAT gives +1.5 for a correct answer, −0.4 for a wrong one and 0 for a blank. IMAT6 scores every practice question the same way, so you learn when a guess is worth it.

Can I plan my study schedule in the app?

Yes. The sky calendar lets you place any sub-topics into your day at a start time, and each study block fills as you finish its questions.

Which devices does IMAT6 run on?

It's designed for iPad in landscape and also runs on iPhone and Mac. Apple Pencil works for handwritten notes. An Android version is planned for Google Play.

Is my progress saved if I change devices?

Yes. Your progress is saved to your account, so when you sign in on another device you start where you left off.

Can I prepare without a tutor?

Yes. IMAT6 is built for self-study: a fixed route through the syllabus, explanations for every option, and mocks that show where you stand.

Is IMAT6 connected to the official exam?

No. IMAT6 is an independent study app and is not affiliated with Cineca or the Italian Ministry of University and Research.
