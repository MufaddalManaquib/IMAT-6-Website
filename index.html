1.1 · Biology · Core

Structure of a water molecule
Water, H₂O, has one oxygen atom joined to two hydrogen atoms by covalent bonds. Oxygen attracts the shared electrons more strongly than hydrogen does, so:
  • oxygen carries a partial negative charge (δ−);
  • each hydrogen carries a partial positive charge (δ+);
  • the molecule is bent (about 104.5°), so the two polar bonds do not cancel. Water is polar.

How it thinks

Every tap is data.

IMAT6 scores you with the exam's own marks, reshuffles questions and options so you can't learn the letters, times every answer, remembers when you weren't sure, and makes the next mock harder when you answer right in under two seconds.

  • +1.5 / −0.4 / 0 on every checked answer
  • Shuffled questions and options, every attempt
  • Timed, so slow answers return in mocks
  • Hold for ! to mark a guess
