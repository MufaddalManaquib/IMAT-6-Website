Devices

Made for iPad.
At home everywhere.

IMAT6 is designed for iPad in landscape, where three questions, a full lesson and the whole sky fit side by side. The same IMAT app turns upright, opens on Mac and fits in your pocket on iPhone, with Android planned for Google Play.

iPad · landscape

The wide screen is the point.

Every screen gets two halves: the day and the month, the lesson and the question, the cover and its folders.

01

Split view.

Lesson on the left, question on the right, terms highlighted between them. On iPhone, the same unit's IMAT traps are a tap away.

02

Apple Pencil.

Write on sticky notes over any screen, highlight, erase, and fade the paper. On iPhone, Notes opens from the crown: type with a finger or write with a pen.

03

The Crown.

A dial at the bottom edge sits under your thumbs when you hold iPad with two hands. On iPhone it opens into Notes, Search, Traps, Marked and Settings.

iPad · portrait

Turn it upright. Then turn it back.

Upright, the folders stack from the bottom and questions come one card at a time. Swipe through them, then turn iPad on its side to open split screen.

Swipe the screen, then turn it on its side for split screen.

Mac

A desk for exam season.

On Mac, the iPad layout opens in a window beside your notes and past papers, with the keyboard and trackpad.
